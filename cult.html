CULT

Cultural Television Channel
Creator, Designer and Motion

Cult is a fictional cultural television channel created during the Master’s in Art Direction. The development of this channel involved an entire process of ideation for its name, concept, and aesthetics, from which the final materials for the brand were created.

The main elements of the channel are the logo, chyrons, and the introductory spot. For this, design and motion design software were used.
