About me

Hello! 👋🏼

I’m Pol Pastor, a graphic designer with a strong background in audiovisual media and advertising art direction. I’ve always been passionate about the creative world, especially cinematography, photography, and design, with a particular focus on post-production.

I hold a degree in Audiovisual Media from TecnoCampus University (UPF) in Barcelona. During my third year, I had the opportunity to enrich my experience by studying at Glyndwr University (Wales) as part of an Erasmus program. Later, I decided to specialize in graphic design by completing a product design program at CIFO, which led me to earn a master’s degree in Advertising Art Direction from Ramon Llull University (Blanquerna). Currently, I am expanding my knowledge in web design and user experience (UI/UX) through an online master’s program at LABASAD.

Throughout my career, I have worked with renowned organizations such as Cuatrecasas, Asepeyo, and Euroleague, collaborating with multidisciplinary teams and developing visual projects across various platforms. I am proficient in tools like Adobe Creative Suite and Figma, enabling me to create innovative and impactful visual content.

My goal is to keep learning and to take part in projects where I can bring value, creativity, and professional dedication.
