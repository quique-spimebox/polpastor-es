“Lattle Battle” Café Branding (2021) 

Branding
Creator and Designer

Latte Battle is a board game café that combines coffee with entertainment. This is a fictitious branding idea created during my studies in the Graphic Product Design course. The project followed all the necessary processes, including Budgeting, Contextualization, Naming, and Applications, among others.

The identity created combines the rules of the game with the uncertainty they produce. This is why there is a combination of the square, symbolizing the game box, with the letters in motion, resulting in the logo.
