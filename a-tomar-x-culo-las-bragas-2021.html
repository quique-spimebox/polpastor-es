A Tomar X Culo Las Bragas (2021)

Comedy short film
Creator, Director, Producer, Graphic Design, and Editing

A Tomar X Culo Las Bragas is a short film written and directed by Ona Sánchez and Pol Pastor. It tells the story of Clara, a 23-year-old bisexual woman who lives in a shared flat with her friend Helena. Clara wants to find love and starts going on dates, but they are always very surreal, and she eventually gets bored of them. However, she doesn’t give up and continues meeting people obsessively until she encounters a date that overwhelms her. One ordinary day, while hanging her underwear, she is surprised by what she will discover from that moment on.

The short film has been nominated at the 2021 Sitges Film Festival in the New Authors SGAE category. It has also received nominations at the 2021 North East International Film Festival, the 2021 AlCurt Festival, and the Sex Education Film Festival in Terrassa.
